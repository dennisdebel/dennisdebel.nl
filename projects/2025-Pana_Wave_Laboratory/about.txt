Pana Wave Laboratory (PWL) is a pseudo scientific tool to make electromagnetic waves audible. Despite its compact frame it outperforms comparable designs in both frequency, range and price due to the custom JM666 chip. Instead of centimeter range, PWL is able to pick up sources from meters away. In its default configuration the bandwidth covers an extremely wide spectrum, spanning at least 50Hz to 7GHz, enabling you to listen to sources ranging from mains power to cellphone GSM signals, WiFi and 5G. The SMA solder pads allow for optional external antennas.<br><br>

<b>Features</b> <br><br>

- Extended bandwidth of at least 50hz - 7ghz Listen to gsm, wifi, bluetooth, 5G. Listen in on phone passcodes, also try hdmi, ethernet etc.<br>
- Sensitive AC and static electricity (you can hear your steps…when you body discharges.<br>
- 100x more sensitive than common emf sniffers (trouble seeker, electroslouch, bitsniffer, soma ether).<br>
- 100x range of common emf sniffers: Near and far field em detection (~200cm range, depending on source signal).<br>
- Loud! No pre amp needed.<br>
- Omni directional antenna for farfield.<br>
- Compact size, great range without the need for big (loop) antennas. <br>
- Multifunctional Volume knob (volume + filter).<br>
- Rejects audio frequency signals (speaker coils) and less microphonic than other sniffers.<br>
- SMA pads for optional external antenna (isolated with jumper).<br>

<b>Warm Tips and tricks</b><br><br>
- Proper headphones with 3.5mm stereo jack recommended, 32 Ohm drivers with 1.5 meter lead recommended.<br>
- You can hear incoming messages on WiFi, GSM, 4G, 5G before they arrive.<br>
- You can hear phone screens (xy?).<br>
- Your are the antenna, every move you make will be audible with this detector. Try walking, try touching metal objects.<br>
- This is a highly experimental project. Feel free to experiment with coils, capacitors and antennas. <br>
- Although mono, you will hear different signals left and right.<br>
- Distance to body changes the capacity and thus the frequency response and range of the receiver, try using your hand as if playing a Theremin.<br>
- If your unit sounds like a 8 bit toy rocket falling from the sky, it is time to change the battery.<br>
- Use the external antenna SMA pads as touch interface.<br>


Pana Wave Laboratory (PWL) 是一款能将电磁波转化为可听声音的伪科学（实验性）工具。尽管外形小巧，但得益于定制的 JM666 芯片，它在频率范围和性价比方面均优于同类设计。PWL 不仅局限于探测厘米级的近距离信号，还能捕捉数米外的信号源。其默认配置下的带宽覆盖范围极广（至少涵盖 50Hz 至 7GHz），使用户能够收听从市电频率到手机 GSM 信号、WiFi 及 5G 信号等各种频段的信号。此外，板载的 SMA 焊盘支持连接外置天线。包装内含：M3 螺丝刀 1 把、M1.

<br><br>

<b>主要特性</b><br>

-扩展带宽：至少 50Hz 至 7GHz。可监测 GSM、Wi-Fi、蓝牙及 5G 信号；甚至能捕捉手机输入密码时的信号，也可尝试监测 HDMI、以太网等接口信号。<br>
-对交流电（AC）和静电高度敏感（甚至能听到脚步声……即身体放电时的声音）。灵敏度是普通 EMF 探测器（如 Trouble Seeker、Electroslouch、Bitsniffer、Soma Ether）的 100 倍。<br>
-探测范围是普通 EMF 探测器的 100 倍：支持近场和远场电磁（EM）检测（探测距离约 200 厘米，具体取决于信号源）。<br>
-音量大！无需前置放大器。<br>
-配备用于远场检测的全向天线。<br>
-体积小巧，无需大型（环形）天线即可实现超长探测距离。<br>
-多功能音量旋钮（集音量调节与滤波功能于一体）。<br>
-能抑制音频频率信号（如扬声器线圈干扰），且相比其他探测器，麦克风效应（microphonics）更低。<br>
-预留 SMA 焊盘，支持连接外置天线（通过跳线进行隔离）。<br>
-您的身体也是电路中的一个有源元件，会影响设备的工作表现。更多详情请参阅下方的“温馨提示与使用技巧”部分。<br>

<br>
<b>温馨提示</b><br><br>

-建议使用配备 3.5mm 立体声插头的优质耳机，推荐规格为 32 欧姆驱动单元及 1.5 米长的线缆。<br>
-在 WiFi、GSM、4G 或 5G 网络下的传入信息到达设备之前，你就能听到相应的信号声。<br>
-你可以听到手机屏幕（产生的电磁干扰）的声音。你自身就是天线的一部分；使用该探测器时，你的每一个动作都会产生可听见的反馈。<br>
-不妨试着走动或触摸金属物体。<br>
-这是一个极具实验性质的项目，欢迎尝试更换线圈、电容器和天线。尽管输出为单声道，你仍能听到左右声道中不同的信号特征。<br>
-人体与设备的距离会改变电容值，进而影响接收器的频率响应和探测范围；试着像演奏特雷门琴（Theremin）那样用手在设备附近移动。<br>
-如果设备发出的声音听起来像是一枚 8-bit 风格的玩具火箭正从空中坠落，那就该更换电池了。<br>
-可以将外部天线的 SMA 焊盘用作触摸感应接口。<br>
