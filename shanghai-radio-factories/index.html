1950-2024

Shanghai Radio Factory Map

An empirical study of Shanghai Radio Factories that operated between 1950-2024. All addresses have been visited, verified and photographed by the author and assistants in between November 2023 and March 2024. The addresses were collected from various online blog posts.

This website is best viewed on a desktop device.

To collapse this menu click the icon. To enable sattelite view click the layers icon. This project is made possible by the generous support of the NYU Shanghai IMA Residency program Fall 2023/Spring 2024.

All data is available in spreadsheet, please contact me for access or more information.

Team: Dennis de Bel (PD0WND), Shengyang Peng (BG8LMR), Qianying Ye.
