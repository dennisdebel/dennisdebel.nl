When Tech Meets Ecology: New 'CareLab' Workshop Brings Critical Making to Campus. <br><br>

As we look for more hands-on ways to engage with sustainability at DKU, we are excited to highlight a unique short series of workshops this spring offered by the Innovation Hub. It's part of the Makers on Site series, titled CareLab: Innovating technologies that sense and care for the living campus. <br>

The workshops are led by Dennis de Bel, an artist and researcher whose work focuses heavily on the intersections between emerging hardware, ecology, and society. The workshops will focus on using technology to monitor and interact with the local micro-climate, plants, and ecosystems right outside our doors.

What sets these workshops apart is their focus on "critical making," which combines hands-on technical
experimentation with interdisciplinary collaboration. Students will work with
microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators to design systems that respond to the environment, although the emphasis goes beyond simple "techno-solutionism." To achieve this, the workshop bring together 13 students from science and engineering, environmental studies, and the arts and humanities. The goal is to foster genuine systems of care that integrate technical skills, ecological knowledge, and creative, human-centered approaches. <br>

Whether students have experience with sensor networks or are simply interested in exploring how art and technology can support our campus ecology, these workshops offer a valuable opportunity to gain hands-on experience and collaborate across disciplines. <br>
